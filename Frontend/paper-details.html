YOLOv8 Computer Vision Smart Cities Deep Learning Public Safety AI Surveillance
Abstract

Urban India faces persistent challenges including littering, stray animal welfare, hit-and-run incidents, and hazardous waste disposal. This research proposes an AI-powered Smart CCTV System that combines YOLOv8 object detection and deep learning techniques to monitor public spaces in real time. The system identifies civic violations, detects injured stray animals, recognizes hit-and-run events, and classifies hazardous waste, generating automated alerts for relevant authorities. By integrating intelligent surveillance with smart city infrastructure, the proposed solution aims to improve public safety, cleanliness, and urban management.
