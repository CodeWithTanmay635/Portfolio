Abstract

Urban India faces four of persistent civic challenges: widespread public littering, a large and often neglected stray animal population estimated around 62 million across the country, large number of Hit & Run cases and growing threat of suspicious waste and hazardous waste thrown in public spaces. The traditional method of civic monitoring, which heavily depend on human patrolling like officers starring at the displays for hours and the stacked files of citizens’ complaints, these all has proven to be inconsistent, and incapable of scaling to the demands of rapid growing Indian cities.

This paper proposes an AI-based Smart CCTV System to tackle four interconnected urban challenges through a unified surveillance platform. Using YOLOv8 (You Only Look Once Version 8) for Object detection and CNNs (Convolutional Neural Networks) for deeper analysis of detected object. The system performs real-time task: detecting civic violation such as littering, monitoring stray animals for injuries or disease, identifying and send alerts regarding Hit & Run cases and classifying the waste hazardous material thrown in the public places is detected. After the detection and deeper analysis, it generates timestamped, GPS-tagged alerts with visual evidence and routes them to relevant authorities via a secure and centralized dashboard. The design of the system is compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the system ensures the encrypted, role-based access through NIC (National Informatics Centre) certified cloud infrastructure.

This research presents the architecture, technical framework, and anticipated impact, demonstrating how AI-powered civic infrastructure can advance the Smart City Mission, Swachh Bharat Abhiyan, and the National Animal Welfare Action Plan, making Indian cities safer, cleaner, and more compassionate.

Keywords: Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, YOLOv8, Smart CCTV, Stray Animal Detection, Littering Detection, Hazardous Waste Classification, Smart City, Urban India, Deep Learning, IoT.
